# Working on the family archive

- Cite the supporting source for genealogical claims. Keep source references in `research/sources/`.
- Record conflicting evidence and unresolved claims in `research/verification/`. Identify uncertain conclusions as such in stories and profiles.
- Use relative links between archive pages so the site works from a local copy or a hosted subdirectory.
- Publish details about living people and private records only with the archive owner's approval.
