Family archive

Sources for Lambson children

These lists show the children identified in the cited records. Some sources describe only part of a household.

Barnabas Lamson and Marie Liggett

Barnabas’s oral will names Mary, Sarah, Barnabas, Martha, and Joseph. The archive discusses the English baptism records for Barnabas Jr. and Martha. The Lambson Family Story · Otis and Frank Lamson, Memorial of Elder Ebenezer Lamson, pp. 15–16

Joseph Lamson Jr. and Elizabeth Adams

The 1908 family memorial names Joseph and David among their sons, alongside Ebenezer. It gives a larger family count than the names identified here. Otis and Frank Lamson, Memorial of Elder Ebenezer Lamson, pp. 16–17

Ebenezer Lamson and Elnathan Lamson

The Bankert genealogy abstracts Ebenezer’s estate and Elnathan’s will and cites Simsbury vital records. Elnathan’s children listed under Jane Hayes follow that compilation. For the children associated with his later household, the archive lists Elnathan as the parent while the identification of his later wife remains open. Bankert genealogy, Ebenezer and Elnathan Lamson entries

Boaz Lambson and Polly Walworth

Lonnie Lambson’s family history identifies Arba Sr.’s siblings using family accounts and census research, including Alfred Boaz Lambson’s account of the family’s move to Michigan. Lonnie Lambson’s family history

Arba Lorenzo Lambson Sr.

Anna Hopkins Lambson’s account identifies her mother as Elizabeth Bonner and names the siblings living when Elizabeth died. The 1861 traveling group places children from both of Arba’s marriages together. The younger children Jonathan, Elizabeth Lillian, and Arba Jr. appear in the household after his marriage to Sarah Harrington. Anna Hopkins Lambson’s account · Church History Biographical Database, 1861 traveling group

Arba Lorenzo Lambson Jr. and Rosella Blanchard

Clara’s autobiography names several siblings. The FamilySearch household summary, which cites the 1900 census, supplies the fuller list, including two children who died in infancy. The 1909 entry supplies no given name. Clara’s account in the Lambson story · FamilySearch household summary
